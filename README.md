# MÉRA Coffee & Kitchen

A premium, modern café website designed to create an elegant digital presence for a contemporary coffee and dining brand.

The website focuses on visual storytelling, smooth interactions, premium typography, responsive layouts, and an immersive café experience.

## Live Demo

https://code-craftstudios.github.io/mera-cafe-website/

## Preview

**MÉRA Coffee & Kitchen**  
Slow coffee. Good food. Better moments.

The website presents the café's identity, signature menu, story, ambience, gallery, customer experience, and contact information through a modern responsive interface.

## Features

- Premium modern café design
- Fully responsive layout
- Animated hero section
- Smooth scrolling and page transitions
- Interactive navigation
- Signature menu showcase
- Café story and timeline
- Experience-based sections
- Image gallery
- Customer testimonials
- Contact and location section
- Table reservation call-to-action
- WhatsApp reservation integration
- Google Maps integration
- Mobile-friendly navigation
- Responsive typography and spacing
- Custom visual assets and illustrations

## Sections

### Home
A visually rich hero section introducing MÉRA Coffee & Kitchen with primary calls to action.

### Menu
Showcases selected coffee, food, and signature items with pricing and visual presentation.

### Our Story
Presents the café's journey and brand story through a timeline-based layout.

### Experience
Highlights different café experiences throughout the day:

- Morning Ritual
- Afternoon Conversations
- Evening Indulgence

### Gallery
A visual showcase of the café environment, food, coffee, and overall ambience.

### Testimonials
Displays customer experiences and feedback.

### Contact
Includes:

- Address
- Opening hours
- Phone number
- Email
- Google Maps
- WhatsApp reservation
